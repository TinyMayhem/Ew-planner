# Baba's U-Dodge | Playable browser beta

- **Entry point:** `/u-dodge.html` (linked from the public Member Den / UTW Chaos Arcade).
- **Branding:** Original U-Bites red/yellow block-U identity. No McDonald's logos, arches, product packaging, or AI API.
- **Art:** The two member-supplied pixel-chibi Baba images are embedded as optimised WebP data URIs in the HTML for a single-file deploy. No uploaded images are stored by the game.
- **Gameplay:** Mobile portrait, drag anywhere to move left/right with a speed cap, Quick Play 60 seconds, Endless Chaos, 3 hearts, 1.5-second hit invincibility, giant burger slows speed by half for 2 seconds, 15-second waves, falling food straight down from the top only, illustrated golden nuggets (not drumsticks), no warning markers, no pause, leaving forfeits.
- **Drops:** Approximately one U-Mystery Box every 15 seconds (at 15s, 30s, and 45s during Quick Play), containing Golden Fries (heal, wasted at full hearts), Mo's Ultimate Slash (clears all food, awards 25 points plus 5 per cleared projectile), or an R4 Shield (one hit, duplicates wasted). Shield blocks damage, slow and combo loss. Golden Fries award +50 points when restoring a heart, and a newly activated R4 Shield awards +50 points. Golden Fries at full health and duplicate shields give no bonus. Power-up bonus points do not increase U-COMBO. A bright above-arena alert names each reward and bonus, including wasted pickups.
- **Scoring:** 10 base points per natural dodge, combo multipliers 1/1.5/2/3 at 0/10/25/50 dodges, 5 survival points per second. Beta uses 250 Quick Play completion bonus pending final balancing.
- **User experience:** Mobile-friendly larger arena with a FULLSCREEN button (native Fullscreen API when supported, CSS fixed-viewport fallback), a canvas-drawn Mo's Katana marked 'MO' with slash sparks; static Baba on the start menu; dramatic start countdown, random silly defeat and victory messages, hit image reactions, optional synth sound and browser speech, menu and immediate rematch, copy results, 10 permanent achievements (7 visible/3 secret), results-only achievement notifications, basic player stats.
- **Public access:** Visitors may open the public Member Den / UTW Chaos Arcade and play without a passcode. Guest players enter a name and are reminded to add an optional alliance tag. UTW member scoring is available only to an active alliance-passcode session; the shared passcode **does not individually verify a person's identity**.
- **Shared scoreboard:** Results from completed runs are submitted through `/api/arcade` to a new, isolated Supabase table `utw_arcade_scores`. The server signs the start session, checks timing and score bounds, rate-limits submissions, and deduplicates attempts; this is a **friendly, not cheat-proof, leaderboard**. Guests and UTW access players are visually distinguished, and both can filter weekly/all-time by game mode. New runs are submitted across devices; existing historical browser-local scores are not migrated. Scores still save in the browser if the shared API is unavailable.
- **Privacy and access:** Buddy Board and Train Calendar data are still protected by the alliance passcode and server-side check, even when linked directly. Guest score submissions never modify the member roster, buddy data, train history, or R4 records. Supabase table has RLS and no client grants. Player names/tags and public scores are visible to anyone on the scoreboard, so visitors should avoid personal information in names.
- **Achievements:** Personal achievement history stays in `localStorage` on the device. The UTW roster name picker remains a demo input for passcode holders, not individually authenticated. Individual member identity and stronger cheat prevention are future enhancements.
- **Testing:** Browser smoke test passed in headless Chromium at a 390×844 viewport, including embedded sprite load, start/menu, countdown, first wave, advancing score and no browser JavaScript exceptions. Additional playtesting and balancing should happen before production release.
- **Deployment:** The beta is live. This revision opens the public arcade, protects private member tools, and adds isolated shared scoring. Test the preview before merging to production.
